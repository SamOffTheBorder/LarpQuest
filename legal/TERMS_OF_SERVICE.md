## 2. What LarpQuest Is

LarpQuest is a collaborative fiction platform. Users create "universes"
(structured settings), run "stories" within them, submit actions as players,
and receive AI-generated prose chapters that advance the narrative based on
those submissions and on stored structured state.

The Service uses third-party artificial intelligence models, accessed through
third-party providers, to perform automated research, generate narrative prose,
validate content against rules, extract structured state, and moderate
submissions.

**The Service is provided for entertainment and creative purposes only.** It is
not a source of factual information, advice, or professional guidance of any
kind.
