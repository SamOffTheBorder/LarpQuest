## Classification

- **Media/genre/tone:** Manga & anime by Masashi Kishimoto. Ninja action-adventure, shonen. Loud, earnest, escalating, with real stakes and real grief — deaths matter and are not undone. Core themes: found family, the cost and cycle of hatred/revenge, what it means to protect people, the gap between a village's ideals and its politics.
- **Primary conflict mode:** Combat (taijutsu/ninjutsu/genjutsu), wrapped in village politics, mission structure, and interpersonal/found-family drama. Investigation and infiltration appear in mission-arc contexts.
- **Power ceiling (full canon, for reference):** Rikudou Sennin-tier (Six Paths) at the top — Naruto/Sasuke post-war, Kaguya, the Sage of Six Paths himself. Below that: tailed-beast jinchuuriki, Kage-tier shinobi, S-rank missing-nin. This campaign's ceiling should be set explicitly once a starting point is chosen (see "Campaign Timeline Anchor" below) — an original genin team starts nowhere near this ceiling and should climb it slowly, the same way LarpQuest/BlackCloverLarp's PCs never approached their setting's top tier.

## Hard Rules

- **Chakra** is the fusion of physical and spiritual energy; everyone with chakra can mold it into jutsu given training. Chakra pools and reserves vary by individual, bloodline, and training — a genin has a small pool relative to a Kage.
- **Chakra natures:** five basic (Fire, Wind, Lightning, Earth, Water) in a rock-paper-scissors-style strength/weakness wheel, plus rarer Kekkei Genkai (bloodline) combinations (e.g. Wood = Earth+Water, Ice = Water+Wind, Lava = Fire+Earth) and Kekkei Tota (three-element fusions). **One or two primary natures per person is standard** — a third or beyond is rare and usually plot-significant, same spirit as BlackCloverLarp's "one attribute per person" rule. New natures/jutsu arrive at narratively earned moments, not on request (see `progression.md`).
- **The three jutsu classes:** Ninjutsu (chakra-shape techniques), Genjutsu (illusion, perception-manipulation), Taijutsu (physical combat, no chakra shaping required though chakra can enhance it). Most shinobi lean toward one or two; a rare few are competent generalists.
- **Kekkei Genkai (bloodline limits)** are inherited, not learned — a clan trait (Sharingan, Byakugan, Wood Release, etc.). **An original PC does not get a canon clan's signature bloodline by default** — if a PC has a bloodline, it should be an original one, distinct from Uchiha/Hyuga/Senju, unless a specific ruling says otherwise.
- **Seals (Fuinjutsu)** are a distinct discipline — sealing chakra, objects, or beings. Powerful and slow to master; not a shortcut.
- **Summoning contracts** bind a shinobi to an animal clan (toads, snakes, slugs, etc.) — acquired through a real in-story event (a trial, an inheritance, a ritual), never simply declared.
- **Tailed beasts (Bijuu) and jinchuuriki** are an S-rank-tier structural fact of the setting — nine exist, each bound to a host. Not something an original genin team should expect proximity to early; treat as background-canon unless a specific arc brings it forward.
- **Death is real and permanent.** Ninja die on missions, in exams, in war. No resurrection outside of specific, rare, costly canon techniques (Edo Tensei, certain seals) — and even those come with steep in-universe costs and are not a tool for undoing a PC death casually.
- **The ninja rank ladder** (Academy Student → Genin → Chunin → Jonin → [Special roles: ANBU, Sannin-tier legends] → Kage) gates authority, mission-class access, and pay grade — not raw power directly, though it correlates. Promotion requires an in-story exam, nomination, or extraordinary act — never simply requested.
- **Mission ranks** (D through S) gate difficulty and payment; D-rank is chores, C-rank is the first real field risk, B/A/S scale up from there. A genin team typically runs D-ranks with occasional C-ranks early on.

## Progression Ladder (Technique)

Basic chakra control (leaf-sticking, tree-walking, water-walking) → learned jutsu within a nature/class → **nature transformation mastery** → combination/original techniques → S-rank signature techniques → legendary/Sage-tier or jinchuuriki-tier abilities (near-mythical; only a handful in the world).

## Rank Ladder

Academy Student → Genin → Chunin → Jonin → (optional: ANBU / special-forces track) → Kage. Advancement comes from exams (the Chunin Exams being the first major canonical gate), missions, and captain/Kage nomination — never simply asking. See `progression.md` for the acquisition rules this campaign runs on.
