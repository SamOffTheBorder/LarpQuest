# Progression Rules

Mirrors LarpQuest's `progression-models` concept and `BlackCloverLarp/rules/progression.md`: every jutsu/technique is tracked at one of three statuses, and advancement requires a narrative cause — never simply asking.

## Status Ladder

- **Mastered** — reliable under pressure, usable at will.
- **Developing** — works, but inconsistent, costly, or situational. Not yet reliable in a real fight.
- **Theoretical** — understood or glimpsed (seen used, read about, partially taught), not yet cast as a working technique.

## How New Capability Is Earned

Consistent with the GM prompt this system is built on, and with how BlackCloverLarp enforces it:

- Training under a mentor (jonin sensei, a specialist, a clan elder) with a stated method
- A real mission or fight forcing a technique into existence under pressure
- Near-death breakthrough
- Discovery of a scroll, sealed technique, or inherited clan knowledge, with a real cost or condition attached
- A summoning contract or bloodline trait surfacing through an actual in-story trigger (never simply declared)

**Intent is not acquisition.** Wanting a jutsu, or a player stating a character "will" learn something, does not make it happen. A request to become dramatically stronger, faster, or to acquire a new nature/bloodline gets evaluated through the Reality Check rule (`house-rules.md`) and, absent a real trigger, comes back "not yet" — with real, earned, bounded gains offered instead, the same shape as BlackCloverLarp's repeated rulings against uncapped power requests (e.g. "ultimate wizard," "Wizard-King-tier speed").

## Awakenings / Major Escalation

Gated behind **real, earned trigger moments only** — never training-montage alone, never simply asking. Depending on the character concept, this might mean: a bloodline trait surfacing for the first time under crisis, a jinchuuriki-adjacent event (only if a campaign ruling establishes a PC has such a connection — not a default assumption), a seal unlocking, or a technique breaking past its normal limit under near-death pressure (the shonen "awakening" beat). Each PC's awakening condition, if any, should be defined privately at character creation (mirroring BlackCloverLarp's approach) and only fires when the story actually produces that condition — not on request.

## House Rules Carried From the GM Prompt

- If a player says a character will do something or succeed, that does not guarantee it happens. Resolve honestly.
- Only quoted text is a character speaking/acting; anything outside quotes is the player talking to the GM.
- Rules questions mid-chapter get answered before the chapter continues.
- Rerolls/revisions are done without argument when requested.
- **"GM override"** is a binding retcon, carried forward as canon from that point on. Track it in `audit/dead-canon.md`, same convention as BlackCloverLarp — the superseded text in `OGFile.md` must be marked void, not silently left in place.
- Characters can die. Injuries persist. Chakra depletes and chakra exhaustion is real (overdraw and you collapse, sometimes for days, same spirit as Black Clover's mana burnout). Resources deplete. Destroyed things stay destroyed. Deaths are permanent.

## Power Ceiling — This Campaign's Open Gate

**Not yet set.** Once the timeline anchor and starting village are pinned (`rules/universe.md`), name this campaign's intended near-term ceiling here (e.g. "Chunin Exam performance," "first S-rank mission," "a specific clan technique") the same way BlackCloverLarp names Mana Zone as its open gate. Until then, treat any request that would push a PC toward Kage-tier, jinchuuriki-tier, or a canon-clan bloodline as a clear "no, with an explanation of what's actually available instead."
