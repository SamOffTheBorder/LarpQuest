# Canon Exceptions

Mirrors LarpQuest's `canon-exceptions` concept and `BlackCloverLarp/canon-exceptions.md`: a log of deliberate, ratified departures from Naruto source canon, distinct from unflagged drift (which lives in `audit/inconsistencies.md` as UNRESOLVED until the user rules on it).

**An entry belongs here only if it was explicitly authorized in-play** — by an opening campaign ruling, a stated GM override, or a direct player instruction. Everything else, no matter how large the divergence, stays in the audit as a decision pending the user's ruling.
